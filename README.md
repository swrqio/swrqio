## Companies I Run

### Tinstra — Founder & CEO
A curated marketplace that makes AI accessible to the businesses that actually run on it — agents, MCP servers, skills, packages, prompt libraries, and templates, all reviewed before they go live. Operators describe what they need in plain language and get matched to a verified, ready-to-run solution (or a custom one). Built end-to-end: architecture, backend, infrastructure, and the agent-deployment layer.

A hospitality technology platform that automates guest check-in, identity verification, traveler registration compliance (RD 933/2021, SES Hospedajes reporting), digital contract signing, and smart-lock integrations for hotels, vacation rentals, and rural accommodations across Spain.
